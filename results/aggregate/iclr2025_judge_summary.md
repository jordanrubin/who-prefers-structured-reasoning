### Key Observations

- **Condition C is stable across all runs**: totals range 26.0–27.7 (sd 0.7). Judges converge tightly.
- **Condition A is bimodal**: runs 2–3 score 24.0–25.3, runs 4–5 score 15.7–16.3. All runs use the same model (opus-4-6), identical prompt files, and same corpus. Runs 4–5 were launched simultaneously as parallel subagents with a slightly different wrapper instruction (more explicit, but structurally different framing). No model downgrade or context truncation occurred. The cause of the ~8-point gap is likely stochastic output variance, possibly amplified by the rubric's sensitivity to depth vs. breadth tradeoffs (runs 4–5 outputs have more sections but fewer words and claims).
- **Assumption Surfacing shows the largest consistent gain** (+1.75): C achieves ceiling (5.0) on every run; A never exceeds 4.0.
- **Corpus Coverage is the only dimension where A matches or exceeds C** (-0.08 mean delta). Both conditions reliably score 4.0+.
- **Falsifiability Yield** is C's floor dimension (uniformly 4.0) and A's weakest (2.0–3.67).
- **Inter-judge agreement is high for C** (runs 4–5 show unanimous scores on all dimensions). Agreement for A is moderate (runs 2–3 show 2-point spreads on some dimensions).

### Fabrication Notes (across all runs)

**Condition A common issues:**
- Garbled/hallucinated paper ID suffixes (flagged in runs 1–3)
- Imprecise quantitative claims ("roughly half," "at least 25")
- Occasional editorial extrapolations beyond corpus evidence

**Condition C common issues:**
- References to prior skill analyses (inductify, negspace, etc.) are not independently verifiable from the corpus alone
- One numerical magnitude error (5 orders vs 3 orders, run 3)
- Minor interpretive stretches on individual paper characterizations

**Neither condition** produced fabricated paper content or invented findings across any run.
